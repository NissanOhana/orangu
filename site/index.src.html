How it works

Three steps. One command each.

Step 1

See

Run npx orangu. It reads every session on your disk, each agent tree included, and it measures with no model. It shows one session, your sessions and their agents, and the whole machine, with the first findings.

$npx orangu

Step 2

Improve

In Claude Code, run /orangu:improve. With no input, 1 prompt runs the whole chain.

  1. Measure. orangu reads your sessions with no model.
  2. Investigate. 2 analysts look for patterns, at high effort.
  3. Interview. If orangu does not know your work yet, it asks about your role. You can skip it.
  4. Suggest. The brain proposes changes to skills, agents, effort, models and scripts.

You approve each change by its id. Then orangu checks it on your later sessions, beyond chance.

>/orangu:improve

Step 3

Tune

Run /orangu:interview at any time to change your answers. The interview asks about your role and how you work. orangu keeps your answers, and the brain ranks each later proposal with them.

>/orangu:interview
Step 1 of 3

What the brain finds

Better results with fewer tokens.

  • Skills

    A skill that never runs, a skill that loads too much text, or a procedure that needs a skill.

  • Agents

    Agents that fail, go too deep or wait. The change that keeps the work and stops the waste.

  • Effort and model

    Deep thinking on simple work. A model that does not fit the task.

  • CLIs and scripts

    The same commands again and again. A search loop that one script can do.

  • Context and cache

    A cache that breaks on each turn. Tools and long instructions that fill the context.

  • Rules that hold

    A rule in CLAUDE.md that the agent breaks. A hook or a deny rule that makes it hold.

Checked, not claimed

Each change is checked on your later sessions.

  1. 01

    No model in the measurement.

    Plain code counts, compares and ranks.

  2. 02

    Beyond chance.

    orangu marks a change verified only when your later sessions beat the earlier sessions beyond chance.

  3. 03

    You approve each change.

    orangu never changes your harness by itself. A change to the whole machine stays a proposal.
